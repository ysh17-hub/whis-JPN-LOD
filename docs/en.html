World Heritage Irrigation Structures in Japan as Linked Open Data

This dataset describes all 60 World Heritage Irrigation Structures (WHIS) in Japan recognized by the International Commission on Irrigation and Drainage (ICID) from 2014 to 2026. Every record cites its sources (official documents of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, prefectures, municipalities and land improvement districts). Values that differ between sources are left blank and both values are recorded in a note.

  • Data: Turtle · JSON-LD · GeoJSON · CSV · VoID · SHACL shapes — licence CC BY 4.0 (photos keep their own licences)
  • URIs: one URI per structure, e.g. https://ysh17-hub.github.io/whis-lod/resource/WHIS-JP-026 (Manno-ike), with HTML, Turtle and JSON-LD.
  • Links: Wikidata (same structure / components / water-source rivers), Linked Open Addresses Japan, e-Stat statistical LOD (standard area codes), FAO AGROVOC (facility types), OpenStreetMap, GeoNames and others.
  • Elevation of each representative point from GSI DEM tiles (1 m mesh first), cross-checked against the GSI elevation service.
  • Map: switch to aerial imagery or a colour relief map and overlay historical aerial photographs (1945–1950, 1961–1969, 1974–1978) from the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan.
